# Onboarding Form — field schema

Build as a Google Form (feeds a Sheet, which Zap 4 watches) or a Notion form. Keep it short — long forms don't get filled out before kickoff.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client Name | Short text | Yes | Should prefill/match the Clients record |
| Company | Short text | Yes | |
| Email | Email | Yes | Used to match the Notion `Clients` record |
| Goals | Paragraph | Yes | "What are you trying to achieve with this engagement?" |
| Budget | Short text | Yes | Confirms/clarifies the package-level budget |
| Timeline | Short text | Yes | "When do you need to see results by?" |
| Success Metrics | Paragraph | Yes | "How will you know this worked?" — feeds directly into the AI Brief's "Success Looks Like…" section |
| Target Audience | Paragraph | No | Who they're trying to reach, if applicable |
| Current Pain Points | Paragraph | No | "What's been frustrating about how this has gone before?" |
| Prior Agency/Tool Experience | Paragraph | No | Context for what to avoid repeating |
| Anything else we should know? | Paragraph | No | Catch-all |

**Send this form link in the Tool Access email or a follow-up, with a due date before the kickoff call** — Zap 4's filter flags (doesn't block) late submissions so the brief can still go out, just with a heads-up to the team.
